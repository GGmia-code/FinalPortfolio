UForecast: Sentiment-Based Campus Weather Mapping

Role

UI/UX Designer, Front-end Developer

duration

January 2024 - April 2024 [4 months]

Tools

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SVG, OpenWeatherMap API
View Live

Project Overview

UForecast is a mood-mapping web app that transforms campus weather and emotional sentiment into a dynamic, interactive experience.It visualizes real-time weather updates across the University of Miami campus and overlays emotional "hotspots" based on how students typically feel in those areas.

The problem

Students often associate certain locations with stress or peace, but those emotional patterns are invisible.
UForecast bridges this gap by mapping emotional associations with physical spaces and layering live weather data for an ambient, informative experience.

Why This App Was Needed

  • Raises emotional awareness on campus
  • Encourages curiosity and exploration
  • Combines utility (weather) with novelty (mood-mapping)
  • Designed to help newcomers, faculty, and students alike
    understand their environment better

Target Audience

  • Students curious about emotional "hotspots"
  • Newcomers and prospective students
  • Faculty interested in student well-being
  • Campus visitors looking to experience the “vibe” of the U

design & Development Process

  • Built iteratively: UI evolved alongside the codebase
  • Used retroactive wireframes to document the structure
  • Real-time user feedback shaped sentiment mapping and overlays
  • Focused on balancing aesthetics with interaction clarity

key features                    

  • Interactive SVG Map with color-coded mood zones
  • Weather Panel using OpenWeatherMap API to display Coral Gables data
  • Sentiment Pop-ups on hover or tap with mood insights
  • Responsive Design — works on desktops and touchscreens

what i learned

  • How to work directly with SVGs and DOM event listeners
  • How to structure a JavaScript-heavy page with visual clarity
  • How to take a concept that is poetic in nature and make it technically expressive
  • The power of design that reflects both mood and utility in real time

reflection

UForecast helped me explore the intersection of technical precision and emotional design. I learned to prioritize clarity and surprise—turning everyday places into ambient, interactive moments. It also reminded me that mapping emotions can be just as important as mapping weather.
