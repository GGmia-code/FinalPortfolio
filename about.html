About Me

Hi, I’m Gargi, a designer, developer, and creative technologist, recently graduated with my B.S. in Computer Science at the University of Miami, with minors in Interactive Media, Chemistry, Math, and Art History.

My work lives at the intersection of technology and creativity, driven by a curiosity for systems and a deep care for people. Over the past few years, I’ve worked on everything from iOS apps and front-end websites to virtual reality experiences and CBLs. I've held roles in instructional design, software quality assurance, academic tutoring, and IT strategy, each one teaching me how to approach problems with structure, empathy, and imagination.

I’ve lived in over nine cities across the U.S. and India, and I identify as a global citizen. This perspective grounds my approach: I care about building things that are intuitive, inclusive, and human.

Outside of work, you’ll find me painting, exploring museums, drinking cardamom chai, or helping international students find community. I believe in thoughtful design, lifelong learning, and digital experiences that make people feel something.

How I see the World:

I see the world as an extension of the inner world. Many of my ideas come to me in dreams or during meditative moments. My design process is intuitive and often nonlinear. I follow sparks of imagination, sketch out the vision I see in my mind, and build from that chaos into clarity. I believe creativity is a form of listening, and design is my way of translating the quiet language of the soul into something others can feel.
